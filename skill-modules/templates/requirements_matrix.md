## Status definitions

- Open: Applicable and awaiting implementation or correction.
- Met: Verified against the current document; record the verification evidence.
- Not applicable: Reviewed and determined not to apply; record the reason.
- Not checked: No verification has been performed; do not imply compliance.
- Blocked: Verification or implementation cannot proceed because a required input, tool, access permission, or dependency is unavailable; record the blocker and next step.
- Needs author input: Cannot be resolved from available evidence without an author decision or missing information.
- Conflict: Two governing sources appear inconsistent.

Use one current status per requirement. Record secondary conditions and dependencies in Notes. Missing evidence or unavailable tools must not be recorded as Met.
