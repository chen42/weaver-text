## Handling notes

Keep supplied files unchanged. Do not create raw-data or derived-data subfolders or generate missing statistical values. Record intake inspections and moves in `log/intake.md`, document edits in `log/edits.md`, and confirmed author decisions in `log/decisions.md`. Those brief logs can refer to this record rather than duplicating it.
